# Simplifying Everything

> Simplicity isn't the absence of depth—it's the removal of unnecessary complexity.

We often mistake complexity for intelligence.

More features, more projects, more responsibilities—they rarely make us better. They make us busier.

The best products, teams, and people focus on doing **fewer things exceptionally well**. Every unnecessary decision steals attention from what truly matters.

The goal isn't to add more.

It's to remove what doesn't.
